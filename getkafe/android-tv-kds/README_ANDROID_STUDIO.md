### 2-Bosqich: APK Faylini Chiqarish (Build APK)
1. Yuqori menyudan **`Build`** menyusiga kiring.
2. **`Build Bundle(s) / APK(s)`** -> **`Build APK(s)`** tugmasini bosing.
3. Bir oz kuting, yig'ilib bo'lgach ekran pastida **`locate`** tugmasi chiqadi.
4. Chiqqan `.apk` fayli (masalan: `app-debug.apk` yoki `app-release.apk`) tayyor bo'ladi!

### 3-Bosqich: Smart TV ga O'rnatish
1. Yaratilgan `.apk` faylini Fleshkaga (USB Flash Drive) nusxalang.
2. Fleshkani Smart TV yoki Android Box televizoringizga suqing.
3. TV dagi **File Manager** (Fayl Yöneticisi) orqali `.apk` faylini bosib o'rnating.
